Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to some common questions about starting an urban garden.

Common Questions

Can I grow food in a small space?

Yes. Pots, buckets designed for growing, window boxes and vertical supports can work well when plants receive suitable light and water.

Which crops are good for beginners?

Leafy greens, spring onions and many herbs are approachable choices. Select varieties suited to your local climate and season.

Do I need a large garden?

No. Start with one or two containers and expand as you learn what works in your space.

How can I take part in Green Urban Farms?

Visit the Get Involved page or use the Contact Us form to ask about current opportunities.
