Beginner Guides

New to urban farming? Start small, observe your plants and build your skills step by step.

What You’ll Learn

  • Choose a sunny growing area
  • Use containers with drainage holes
  • Start with easy crops such as leafy greens and herbs
  • Water consistently and check plants regularly
